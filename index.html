What we provide

CozyTV+ provides software functionality, application access, activation and related support services.

Paid plans, when offered, relate to access to CozyTV+ software features and services.

What we do not provide

CozyTV+ does not provide, host, sell or distribute IPTV subscriptions, TV channels, playlists, movies, series or other third-party audiovisual content.

Users are responsible for ensuring they have the necessary rights to access any media source they add to the application.
