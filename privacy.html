CozyTV+ Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026

Applies to: CozyTV+ for LG webOS, Android / Google Play, and iOS / Apple App Store

1. About CozyTV+

CozyTV+ (“CozyTV+”, “the App”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) is a media and IPTV player that allows users to play television channels, movies, series, electronic program guide data, subtitles, and other media from sources added by the user.

CozyTV+ does not provide, host, create, sell, retransmit, or distribute IPTV subscriptions, television channels, movies, series, playlists, streaming packages, or other audiovisual content. M3U/M3U8 playlists, Xtream-compatible accounts, XMLTV/EPG sources, direct stream links, subtitle services, and similar sources are added or configured by the user.

CozyTV+ is developed and operated under the brand name Cosmo Online.

2. Information CozyTV+ Processes

CozyTV+ does not require a separate CozyTV+ account solely for basic IPTV playback. Credentials entered for IPTV, EPG, subtitle, or other third-party services belong to those third-party services.

2.1 Source and service credentials

  • M3U/M3U8 playlist URLs and uploaded M3U files;
  • Xtream-compatible server addresses, usernames, and passwords;
  • XMLTV / EPG URLs and direct stream URLs;
  • subtitle-service credentials, API keys, session tokens, or identifiers.

These details are used to connect to services or sources selected by the user and to retrieve or play requested content.

2.2 Preferences and viewing-related information

  • favorites, recently viewed channels, watchlists, and playback position;
  • reminders, hidden or reordered channels, and layout preferences;
  • language, theme, text-size, time-zone, audio, and subtitle preferences.

2.3 Parental-control information

If parental controls are enabled, CozyTV+ may store access-restriction settings and a salted hash of the parental-control PIN.

2.4 Device, application, and technical information

  • platform and operating-system version;
  • App version and playback-related device capabilities;
  • network connection state and player capabilities;
  • locally generated error and performance information;
  • information required for timers, reminders, or notifications.

CozyTV+ does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or precise-location permission for its core IPTV-player functionality.

2.5 License, trial, and purchase status

Where device activation, a trial, subscription, or purchase system is used, CozyTV+ may process an application-generated CozyTV+ Device ID or equivalent platform-safe identifier, trial start/expiry information, activation and license status, product/subscription entitlement, purchase-restoration status, and limited technical information necessary to prevent unauthorized activation or abuse.

CozyTV+ should not use a hardware MAC address as a general-purpose user tracking identifier where an application-generated identifier or platform-supported entitlement mechanism is available.

Payments made through Google Play or Apple’s App Store are processed by the relevant store provider. CozyTV+ does not receive full payment-card details from those stores.

2.6 Support information

If you contact support, we may receive information you choose to provide, such as your email address, issue description, device or App version, screenshots, logs, or CozyTV+ Device ID where needed for activation support.

3. How Information Is Collected

Information may be obtained from information and files you enter or upload, actions inside the App, technical information exposed by the device or operating system, responses from services you choose to use, and store entitlement systems on Google Play, Apple App Store, or supported LG distribution/payment systems.

4. Local Storage and Developer Access

Account settings, preferences, viewing-related information, and locally imported sources are primarily stored in the App’s storage on the device, including local application storage, a local database, browser or WebView storage, platform preferences, or local file storage.

In the current architecture, CozyTV+ does not rely on a developer-operated cloud service to synchronize viewing history, favorites, playlists, or IPTV credentials across devices unless such a feature is explicitly introduced and disclosed in a later version of this Policy.

CozyTV+ does not sell personal data and does not use IPTV viewing data for third-party advertising profiling.

5. IPTV, EPG, Image, and Media Servers

When you add or use IPTV, EPG, artwork, metadata, streaming, or other media sources, the relevant third-party server may receive your IP address, requested URL or resource, request date and time, technical network headers, and credentials or access tokens when authentication is required.

Your IPTV or media provider may therefore be able to determine which resources you request or play. CozyTV+ does not control the privacy, security, availability, or legality of third-party IPTV or media providers.

6. Online Subtitle Services

Depending on configured features, CozyTV+ may connect to subtitle services such as SubDL, OpenSubtitles, Assrt, or other user-selected providers. Requests may include title/search text, content identifiers, season and episode information, language preferences, API keys, subtitle identifiers, and service-specific account credentials or session tokens where required.

7. Local-Network Setup

CozyTV+ may allow a phone or computer on the same local network to send source information or upload M3U files to the TV or device. Access may be protected by a pairing code and time-limited session token. If a local setup connection uses plain HTTP rather than HTTPS/TLS, traffic is not cryptographically protected in transit. Use this feature only on a trusted local network.

8. Platform-Specific Processing

8.1 LG webOS

On LG webOS, CozyTV+ may use platform storage, network access, media-playback capabilities, and system scheduling/timer functions needed for reminders or playback. If CozyTV+ uses its own device activation or licensing system on LG webOS, entitlement information may be sent to the CozyTV+ licensing service solely to determine trial or license status and prevent unauthorized activation.

8.2 Android / Google Play

On Android, CozyTV+ may use Internet access, network-state access, wake-lock or equivalent functionality, local application storage, and Google Play entitlement or billing APIs when purchases or subscriptions are offered.

CozyTV+ does not currently state that it uses Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or RevenueCat. If such an SDK is added, this Policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration must be updated before release.

Android system backup or device-to-device transfer may include some App data depending on operating-system version, device manufacturer, and backup settings.

8.3 iOS / Apple App Store

On iOS, CozyTV+ may use Apple-provided local application storage, network access for user-selected sources, StoreKit / App Store purchase-entitlement mechanisms, and platform features needed for playback, settings, and permitted notifications or reminders.

Any data collected through an iOS version of CozyTV+, including by integrated third-party SDKs, must also be disclosed accurately in App Store Connect App Privacy information.

9. Advertising and Analytics

The current CozyTV+ policy does not assume that the App contains third-party advertising, cross-app tracking, or automatic remote analytics. If advertising, behavioral analytics, attribution, or remote crash-reporting services are introduced, this Policy will be updated to identify the provider, data categories, processing purposes, tracking/linking status, and available consent or opt-out controls.

10. Security

CozyTV+ uses reasonable technical measures appropriate to the App’s architecture, including operating-system application isolation and access controls. IPTV or subtitle passwords may be stored in local App storage and are not necessarily protected by additional application-level encryption. HTTP sources do not provide transport encryption; HTTPS should be used whenever supported. Diagnostic logs may contain URLs, content identifiers, or connection information.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Local sources, preferences, and settings remain on the device until changed, removed, reset, or deleted according to the relevant feature. Temporary data such as cached channels, EPG data, artwork, metadata, subtitle files, and availability results may be removed according to cache limits, expiry rules, or cleanup operations.

  • Clear Cache: removes temporary downloaded or processed data but may not remove accounts, preferences, viewing history, or original imported files;
  • Reset App: removes App-managed local settings, personal usage records, and App-managed cache;
  • Delete imported files: imported playlist files may need to be removed separately;
  • Platform storage controls: users may clear App data, uninstall the App, or manage platform backups through the operating system.

Where entitlement or licensing records are stored on a CozyTV+ licensing service, they may be retained as necessary to provide the license, restore purchases or activation, prevent fraud and unauthorized reuse, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Where applicable, users may request deletion of personal information held by the developer by contacting the privacy address below.

12. Third-Party Services and International Transfers

Third-party IPTV providers, subtitle services, app stores, payment systems, hosting providers, or licensing infrastructure may operate servers outside the user’s country. Independent third parties process data under their own privacy policies. Where CozyTV+ itself transfers personal information internationally, such transfers will be handled in accordance with applicable data-protection law and required safeguards.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights to request information about personal data processed about you; access, correction, deletion, or restriction; object to certain processing; withdraw consent where applicable; request data portability; and complain to a competent data-protection authority.

Where Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (“KVKK”) applies, users may also exercise the rights granted under Article 11 of the KVKK, subject to applicable conditions.

Use of CozyTV+ by itself is not treated as consent for processing that legally requires explicit consent.

14. Children’s Privacy

CozyTV+ is not designed to collect a child’s name, date of birth, or contact information through a dedicated child account-registration process. Media accessible through CozyTV+ comes from sources selected by the user. Parents and guardians are responsible for controlling sources and content made available to children.

15. IPTV and Content Responsibility

CozyTV+ is a media player and does not verify, license, endorse, or supply content contained in user-added sources. Users are responsible for ensuring they are authorized to access or stream sources they add, complying with copyright and licensing requirements, and obtaining any necessary subscriptions or permissions from the relevant content provider.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when CozyTV+ features, platform integrations, legal requirements, or data-processing practices change. The effective date at the top will be updated when a new version is published.
